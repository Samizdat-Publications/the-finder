# M5: The pictures complete, and The Voices

_Written by the lead in build session 2 (2026-10-16), while M4's audit fixes were in flight. Effort: high._

**Goal:** the record's two halves read in full. The Pictures go on from the first frames to all 156, in
colour where the tape carries colour, turned the right way up, and the teaching pictures read with the
senders' own numerals and units: from them the player learns the senders' counting, their units, their
star's family of planets (the third world is home) and how tall they are. The Voices opens the rest of the
record: greetings, the sounds of their world and their music, mostly to listen to, with a few deductions
that come from the sound itself.

**Done when (PLAN.md M5):** the bot completes both chapters through the real interface (and a wrong-answer
bot confirms each meaningful wrong result); every frame decodes in the browser from the tape; listening
works in Chrome and Edge (Firefox: see "Decisions to make"); critics at the gate; a preview link to
Stewart.

**Read first:** `PLAN.md` chapters 6 and 7; `docs/research/images-and-audio.md`; `data/frames.json`
(156 frames: titles, `colourRole` R/G/B for the 20 colour triples, `orientationBarry` 0/1/2) and
`data/record.json` (the two sides, D-A17's break); D-A04, D-A05, D-A08 (colour order shown as probable),
D-A17, D-A18, D-A19; the M3 Pictures chapter (`src/chapters/pictures/`) and the M2 decoder and turntable;
`docs/reviews/2026-10-16-M3-player-critic.md` (the pictures scored 8/10: keep what works) and the M4
reviews.

## The design rule (from M3's review, as in M4)

Deduce on the instrument, do not pick from a list; every clue the most legible thing on screen; every
number from the tape, the audio or the science; a wrong answer shows its computed result. And one new
rule from the critic: **give the alien script a job.** The teaching pictures are where the senders'
numerals are learned; let the player build the table from the senders' numerals to the Finder's own (the
codex's glyph numerals), so the alien script becomes a tool the player reads with, not a skin.

## What the player does

**The Pictures, continued (about 25 minutes plus browsing).**
- **The album:** every frame decodes from the tape as the record plays, with the settings found in M3;
  an album of the decoded frames (thumbnails decoded, never the clean images: rule 2).
- **Colour:** some pictures come three times in a row with different shades (`colourRole`); the player
  stacks a triple as three primaries and sees a colour picture; the order of the three is shown as
  "probable" (D-A08: the record cannot prove it), judged by which order makes known things look as the
  senders' other pictures say (the solar spectrum frame L007 to L009 is the natural test: a spectrum's
  colours run in order).
- **Down:** some frames come out turned (`orientationBarry`); the player turns them until people stand
  and water lies flat; the checker compares with the orientation measured in M1 (data, not typed).
- **Their numerals** (L002, Mathematical definitions): dots and symbols count up; the player builds the
  table from the senders' digits to their own glyph numerals (the alien-script puzzle).
- **Their units** (L003, Physical unit definitions): the hydrogen key again, now in the senders' own
  notation: length, mass and time defined; read with the table.
- **Their star's planets** (L004, L005, Solar System parameters): with numerals and units, read the
  distances and masses; the third world is marked: home. Check against the planets' real values.
- **How tall they are** (L039, Diagram of male and female; L050, family ages): heights in the units
  just learned: about 1.7 to 1.8 m, computed from the decoded frame's own marks.
- Codex: their counting, their units, their home world, their size.

**The Voices (about 15 minutes, open-ended).** The rest of the record on the turntable, side by side with
a spectrogram (a pure FFT in `src/dsp`) and a pitch tracker; mostly listening. Deductions from the sound,
each checked against the real audio:
- **How many voices greet you:** segment the greetings by their pauses and count the languages; check
  against the published list (55 languages, research).
- **What they hear:** the record's sound spans a band of frequencies (measured across the music); the
  senders hear about 20 Hz to about 15 kHz.
- **How their music is built:** pitch ratios in a piece cluster at 2:1 and 3:2 (octaves and fifths);
  their scale steps are near 2^(1/12).
- **Not only their voices:** whale song among the greetings, and the Sounds of Earth told as a story
  (from weather and geology to life to machines): order the sounds.
- The rest is the reward: the 27 pieces of music, with the record's own order and side break shown as
  an inference (D-A17).

## Steps (specs in `docs/plans/M5_specs/` before delegating)
1. **Research** (scout, then the lead): the teaching frames (what each states, in what notation; how
   an alien reads them; the values they encode, checked on our decoded frames), the colour order and
   the orientation data's provenance; The Voices (the segmentation ground truth for the greetings, the
   band of the recorded sound, pitch-tracking method and its error, the Sounds of Earth sequence, the
   whale song's place). `docs/research/teaching-frames.md`, `docs/research/voices.md`.
2. **Science and DSP:** colour assembly (pure, from three decoded frames), the orientation check, a
   numeral reader for the counting frame (the decoded frame's own dots), the planet table, the height
   measure; an FFT spectrogram and pitch tracker in `src/dsp` (tested against synthetic tones and the
   real tracks), the greetings segmenter (tested against the research count).
3. **The Pictures complete** (the album, colour, down, numerals, units, planets, height).
4. **The Voices** (the turntable for the rest of the record, the spectrogram, the deductions).
5. **Graph and gating:** the new nodes with spoiler-free questions and three-tier hints; The Voices opens
   after `player.firstSound` (already in the graph); Portrait (M6) needs the pictures' home world and
   height.
6. **Sound, bots, captures, critics:** the album and the voices in the cue system; bot.game extended to
   the new nodes; captures; the player-critic; an Edge browser run.
7. **Gate:** science audit, QA audit, PROGRESS and HANDOFF, `docs/plans/M6.md`.
