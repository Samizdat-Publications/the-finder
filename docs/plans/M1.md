# M1: Ground truth

**Goal:** prove the hard parts with real data before any game code. At the end: the real 1977 image
tape decodes (calibration circle round, all 156 frames on a contact sheet), every etched number on the
cover is transcribed and verified, and the science core computes every "when" and "where" deduction
with honest error bars, tested against the research numbers. No interface yet.

**Read first:** `PLAN.md` ("The experience", "How it's checked", "Architecture"), `docs/research/README.md`
(the cross-report corrections), then the research file each step names.

**Rules that bite here:** real data only (CLAUDE.md rule 1); downloads only from named sources with
checksums (rule 9); science code pure and deterministic (rules 4, 6); tests compare against the research
numbers, never against the code's own output.

## Step 1: the reference decoder (Python; `builder-opus`, lead reviews every picture)

      fails). Read the WAV in chunks; never load 1.46 GB at once.
- [ ] Frame starts per channel (left: images 1 to 54, right: 55 to 116; first frames near 15.63 s and
      16.94 s); validate every detected start against Barry's table.
- [ ] Line sync locked to the **falling edge** with the two alternating pulse widths (the cover's
      staggered interlace); per-frame line-period fit (the cover says 8.3396 ms; the tape runs 0.16% fast);
      droop (AC-coupling) inverse; polarity (more signal is darker).
- [ ] Aspect from the calibration circle (frame L000): fit an ellipse to the circle's edge and choose the
      horizontal scale that makes the axis ratio 1.00 (expect a frame aspect near 4:3).
- [ ] Outputs (gitignored `build/ref/`): 156 grayscale frames, the 20 colour composites (frames listed in
      `docs/research/data/images/image_list_116.txt`, R,G,B order as an inference), `metrics.json`
      (circle axis ratio, residual slant per frame, sync-lock jitter), a 156-frame contact sheet, and the
      circle with its fitted ellipse drawn on.
- [ ] Meaningful-wrong-result proofs: decode the circle with the line time off by 1%, 5% and 20% and with
      the interlace offset dropped; record the measured slant and comb artifacts in `metrics.json`; these
      become the M2 browser tests.
- [ ] Also write the 96 kHz, 16-bit, mono per-frame slices the browser will use (`build/slices/`), with an

## Step 4: the science core (`src/science/`; `builder-opus` per module, lead writes each spec)

Read `docs/research/clocks.md` and `cover.md` first. All modules pure TypeScript, seeded randomness.

- [ ] `units.ts`: hydrogen constants (1,420,405,751.768 Hz; 0.70402418376 ns; 21.106114054 cm), binary
      parsing.
- [ ] `epoch.ts`: projects real data to the story epoch: stars by linear motion; pulsars by spin-down
      with braking index (Crab 2.342, Vela about 1.7, others 3) plus seeded timing noise and glitches with
      documented amplitudes; proper motion; the craft along its asymptote; the observer's frame.
- [ ] `pulsars.ts`: identification (period and direction), spin-down forward and inverse, glitch
      correction, observer-frame Doppler correction (fit or known frame), light time.
- [ ] `triangulation.ts`: fit the map's origin from the 14 bearings (robust least squares, angle sigma
      from the measured map residuals, distances as weak constraints), with a Monte Carlo region.
- [ ] `clocks.ts`: Bateman chains for the uranium spot (pure U-238 start; U-234 and Th-230 ingrowth) and
      the RTG fuel (Pu-238 to U-234 to Th-230 to Ra-226), simulated measurements with Poisson or
      mass-spectrometry noise, inversion with confidence intervals; works at 49 years and at 44,000.
- [ ] `kinematics.ts`: for each star within 10 pc of the craft, the angle between the craft's velocity
      relative to that star and the direction from that star; distance over speed.
- [ ] **Tests (Vitest), against the research numbers:**
  - [ ] Map dating today: Crab 1971.76, glitch-corrected Vela 1971.77 (each within 0.05 yr); 13-pulsar
        mean 1969.7 (within 0.3), spread about 1.4; B1240-64 flagged unusable.
  - [ ] Identification is unique at 2026 and at the story epoch.
  - [ ] Triangulation: the region contains the Sun; report its size (Sagan's design claim: about 20 pc).
  - [ ] Story-epoch dating in the Sun's frame lands within its honest interval of the true elapsed time;
        without the reframe, the old pulsars scatter by the predicted amount.
  - [ ] Clocks: 1,000 seeded trials each; the 95% interval contains the truth 93 to 97% of the time; at
        49 years and at 44,000 years.
  - [ ] Kinematics: exactly one star (the Sun) has the craft moving radially away (angle under 0.1 deg);
        distance over speed matches the elapsed time.
  - [ ] Ephemeris: spot checks against fresh Horizons values within 100 km.
- [ ] **The neighbour twist:** list Gliese 445's nearest stars at the story epoch and the Sun's apparent
      magnitude from Gliese 445 and from the craft. If the Sun is not among its nearest neighbours, say so,
      log a decision, and adjust the story text; never adjust the data.
