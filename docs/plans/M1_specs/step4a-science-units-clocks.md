## Modules

### `units.ts`
- Hydrogen hyperfine constants with sources in comments: `HYDROGEN_HZ = 1_420_405_751.768` (maser value, clocks.md section 5), `T_H_SECONDS = 1 / HYDROGEN_HZ` (0.70402418376 ns), `LAMBDA_H_METRES = C / HYDROGEN_HZ` (21.106114054 cm), and `SAGAN_HYDROGEN_HZ = 1.420405752e9` (the value Sagan, Sagan and Drake 1972 used; cover.md "Units").
- `parseCoverBinary(s: string): number` for strings of '0'/'1' up to 53 bits (throw on anything else or longer); `binaryToSeconds(s, hz = HYDROGEN_HZ)`; `binaryToMetres(s)` (the length reading, for the "times, not lengths" deduction).
- Physical constants used elsewhere: speed of light (km/s), Julian year (s), parsec (km), AU (km), Avogadro, MeV in joules; each with a source comment (CODATA/IAU).
- Tests (`units.test.ts`): the rotation string `100110000110010000000000000000000` gives 5,113,380,864 and 3.59994 s (tolerance 1e-5 s; cover.md 1b); the 43-bit play time `1000010110000000000000000000000000000000000` gives 4,587,025,072,128 and 3229.4 s (+/- 0.05 s) = 53.82 min; the 24-bit line time `101101001100000000000000` gives 11,845,632 and 8.3396 ms (+/- 0.0001 ms); `1000000000` gives 512. Read as a length, the rotation number is about 1.08e9 m (a million kilometres) for a 30 cm disc: assert it exceeds 1e8 m while the time reading is 3.6 s. ### `random.ts`
Seeded streams only. `createRng(seed: string | number)` returning `{ uniform(): number in [0,1); normal(): number; poisson(lambda: number): number; fork(label: string): Rng }`. Use a well-known small generator (sfc32 or xoshiro128**) seeded through a string hash (for example cyrb128 or splitmix32), Box-Muller or Marsaglia polar for normals (cache the pair deterministically), and an exact Poisson sampler: inversion for small lambda and PTRS (Hormann 1993) for larger; for lambda above about 1e7 a normal approximation is acceptable with a comment stating the error. `fork(label)` gives an independent, reproducible sub-stream. Tests: the same seed gives the same sequence; different labels differ; uniform mean and variance, normal mean and variance and Poisson mean and variance at lambda = 3, 50, 1e4, 1e6 within sampling tolerances computed from n (not chosen).

### `stats.ts`
`normalQuantile(p)` (Acklam or Wichura AS241), `normalCdf`, `chiSquareCdf(x, k)` (regularized incomplete gamma), `wilsonInterval(k, n, conf)`, `clopperPearson(k, n, conf)` (via the regularized incomplete beta), `brentRoot(f, lo, hi, tol)` (or bisection with a guarantee), and small helpers (mean, sd, weighted mean with error). Test against published values (for example normalQuantile(0.975) = 1.959963985; chi-square 95th percentile with 1 dof = 3.841458821; known Clopper-Pearson examples), with sources in comments.

### `clocks.ts`
- A general linear-chain Bateman solver (any initial amounts of every member, distinct decay constants), numerically safe for half-lives from 14 years to 4.5e9 years at t from 0 to 1e6 years (verify against an independent method in a test: high-order numerical integration of the ODE system with small steps). Activities in Bq.
- **Uranium spot** (clocks.md 4a): 0.00026 microcurie of U-238 (9.62 Bq; NASA golden-record-cover page) electroplated onto a 2 cm spot; assumed isotopically pure at t = 0 (NASA's "ultra-pure"; a documented assumption). Chain U-238 to U-234 to Th-230 to Ra-226 (Th-234 and Pa-234m in equilibrium within months; ignore them, with a comment). `uraniumSpotState(tYears, { initialA234overA238 = 0 })` returns atoms, activities and the activity ratios A234/A238, A230/A238, A226/A238.
- **Counting measurement:** `simulateAlphaCounts(state, { countingTimeS, efficiency }, rng)` returns Poisson counts in the U-238, U-234 and Th-230 lines, assuming the uranium and thorium fractions are separated chemically before counting (so Ra-226 does not overlap U-234; say so in a comment).
- **Inversion:** `ageFromU234(counts)`: age and a 95% interval assuming a pure start: conditional on the total of the two uranium lines, the U-234 count is binomial with p = R/(1+R) where R = A234/A238; take a score (Wilson) or likelihood-ratio interval for p and map it through the monotonic R(t) by root finding. `fitAgeAndPurity(counts)`: two unknowns (age and the initial A234/A238) from the three lines by maximum likelihood (multinomial conditional on the total), with a profile-likelihood 95% interval on the age and a goodness-of-fit test of the pure-start hypothesis (p-value). It must say "not determinable" honestly when Th-230 counts are too few (at 49 years Th-230 gives one decay every 39 days).
- **Wrong starting assumption:** `testStartHypothesis(counts, initialA234overA238)` returns a p-value; assuming chemically purified natural uranium (initial A234/A238 = 1) must be rejected decisively by the 44,000-year measurement, while the pure start is accepted.
- **RTG fuel** (clocks.md 4b): three MHW-RTGs per spacecraft, 24 PuO2 spheres each of 251.5 g, plutonium 80 at.% Pu-238 (LA-5202), giving about 12.8 kg Pu-238 per spacecraft (derive it in code from those inputs and the PuO2 molar mass; the research derived 4.25 kg per RTG). The remaining 20 at.% is a documented assumption: use the isotopic mix of the named CASSINI source (https://fti.neep.wisc.edu/fti.neep.wisc.edu/neep602/SPRING00/lecture6.pdf ; WebFetch it and cite the numbers; if it does not give a full vector, use LA-5202 if it does, and otherwise stop and report which values you could not source). Chains: Pu-238 to U-234 to Th-230 to Ra-226; Pu-239 to U-235; Pu-240 to U-236; Pu-241 to Am-241 to Np-237; Pu-242 to U-238. `rtgState(tYears)` returns masses (kg), activities, ratios (Th-230/U-234 activity and atom ratios, U-234/Pu-238, U-238/U-234 atom ratio) and decay heat (W, assuming radon is retained; document the energy model). `simulateRtgMeasurement(state, { relativePrecision }, rng)` (mass-spectrometry atom ratio with Gaussian relative error) and `ageFromTh230U234(measurement)` with a 95% interval; the half-life systematic reported separately by re-inverting with the Cheng 2013 set.
- **Wrong decay chain:** `ageSolutions(measurement, chainHypothesis)`: for the true chain (Pu-238 parent) an age exists that fits both the Th-230/U-234 and the Ra-226/Th-230 ratios; for the hypothesis "the U-234 came from natural uranium" (U-234 in equilibrium with U-238 that is absent) there is no consistent solution. Keep the API small; the game's Arrival chapter will use it later.
