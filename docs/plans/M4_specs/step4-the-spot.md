## The science you add (`src/science/spot.ts`, pure, no DOM, seeded streams only)
- **The detector.** A circular silicon alpha detector of active area A at a stand-off h above the cover, counting alphas from the spot (a uniform disc, `data/spot.json` diameter, all four chain members' activities from `uraniumSpotState(age)` at the discovery epoch's `yearsAfterLaunch`). The count rate at a probe offset r from the spot's centre is the activity per area times the solid-angle fraction of the detector seen from each point of the disc, integrated over the disc (numerical quadrature; the deposit is ultra-thin, so no self-absorption: say so). The rate depends only on r: build it as a 1D table once (for example 0 to 120 mm in 0.5 mm steps) and interpolate. Test it against the on-axis point-source limit, the closed form 2 pi (1 - h / sqrt(h^2 + R^2)) / (4 pi), and against 1 / r^2 far away (check ids `spot.detector.onAxis`, `spot.detector.farField`).
- **The detector's numbers** (A, h, and its background in the alpha window) are a documented game choice: take them from a commercial alpha-spectroscopy silicon detector's published datasheet (for example a passivated implanted planar silicon detector of a few hundred mm^2 with a background of order 0.05 counts per hour above 3 MeV), confirmed with a web search, cited in `docs/research/alpha-detector.md` (URL, date read, the values) and `docs/CREDITS.md`, and logged as D-A24 with a revert note. If you cannot confirm a value, say so in the note and the report and mark it UNVERIFIED; the background barely matters (the spot gives counts per second; say how much it matters, computed).
- **The sweep**: counts in a dwell at a probe position: Poisson(rate(r) x dwell + background x dwell), drawn from the seed's stream `spot/sweep` (advancing per sample: the same path gives the same counts).
- **The counting session** (for the age and purity): the player counts a scraping of the spot after separating uranium from thorium (clocks.ts's documented assumption), on the same detector at its datasheet geometry. Counts accumulate in fixed quanta of counting time (for example one hour), each quantum's three line counts drawn in order from the seed's stream `spot/count`, so the counts at a total time are the same however the player got there and counting longer only ever adds counts. Expose `countsAt(totalSeconds): AlphaCounts` with the detector efficiency computed from the geometry (never typed). Test determinism and monotonicity (check id `spot.counting.deterministic`).

- **The purity test** (after `spot.age`): a dial for the starting U-234 (A234/A238 at the start, from 0 to a little beyond 1: natural uranium sits in secular equilibrium, an activity ratio of 1, which the code derives from the half-lives the way `src/chapters/arrival/chains.ts` does, never typed); for the dialled start, the instrument shows the age each clock gives (the U-234 clock and the Th-230 clock, from the counts) and whether they agree (`testStartHypothesis`'s p-value as a plain-language agreement meter).

## The three checkers (pure, in `checkers.ts`; every wrong answer reports what it computed)
1. `spot.find`, answer `{ xMm, yMm }` (cover millimetres): OK when the point lies inside the spot's disc (data/spot.json). Wrong: the count rate expected there against the background, both computed ("nothing above background here").
2. `spot.age`, answer `{ countingTimeS, ageYears }`: the checker recomputes `countsAt(countingTimeS)` from the seed (not trusting the client), runs `ageFromU234`, and is OK when `ageYears` lies inside its 95% interval and the interval's lower end is above zero (the spot is measurably old). Wrong: the interval, and if the lower end is zero, "count longer: this few U-234 counts cannot tell an old spot from a new one". Result readings: the age and its 95% interval (statistical and the half-life systematic), the counting time, the counts.
3. `spot.purity`, answer `{ countingTimeS, startA234overA238 }`: OK when `testStartHypothesis(counts, start).pValue >= 0.05` and the natural start is rejected with the same counts (`pValue < 0.05` at the natural activity ratio of 1). Wrong when the start is rejected (its p-value and the two clocks' ages); when the natural start is not yet rejected: "count longer: with this few thorium counts both starts fit". Result readings: the start, the fitted age from `fitAgeAndPurity`, both p-values.
