## The instrument (one screen at 1440x900 and 1366x768; follow the layout conventions the M3 layout pass left in `src/ui/` and the M3 chapters' CSS)
- **The galaxy chart** (`mountGalaxy` with the model's `alien` and `measured`: pass both so it computes nothing twice) fills most of the instrument.
- **The cover's map** beside it (the `#map` group of the cover SVG, `src/chapters/shared/coverSvg.ts`): the 14 lines and the long Galactic-centre line, each line selectable. The selected line's binary number is read through the key the player found (show it as a time in the alien unit; with the human gloss on, the time in seconds in parentheses: `src/ui/units.ts`).
- **The overlay**: the map drawn on the chart (`showMap` with a transform). At first the map is unaligned; the player turns it (drag or a dial) until the long line points at the Galactic centre; then the rays point along the sky toward their pulsars. Show the angle between the long line and the centre direction while turning; no snapping. Later (`map.region`) the player also slides the map's origin and scales it.
- **Assigning**: select a line, then click a pulsar on the chart (`pick`). The assignment is drawn as a link (`setMatches`) and the panel shows, for that line and that pulsar, computed readouts: the map's period, the pulsar's period now, the direction offset (deg), the latitude offset where the tick resolves (`tickLatitudeDeg`), and the **match quality** from the model's `identification()`: the candidate's log10 odds against all the others on that line (`LineMatch.ranked`, D-A16), written as plain odds ("about 10^5 to 1", with the units helper's powers of ten). A pulsar outside the line's preselection gets the reason it was not considered (direction off by more than the map allows, or no age the Arrival clock allows makes it reach the map's number). Young lines (pass 2 needs the old lines' age) show "match quality needs the map's age first" until three old lines are matched.
- **The spin-down clock** (after `map.periods`): a time slider from about 100,000 years ago to 100,000 years ahead; each matched line's pulsar period runs with it (`spinForward` from `src/science/pulsars.ts` with n = 3, `N_NOMINAL`), drawn against the map's number; where the curves cross the map's number is where that pulsar agrees with the map.
- **Ages across the sky** (after `map.identify`): the old lines' ages in the alien's own frame (`ageOf(order, jname, 'own')`) on a sky plot (galactic longitude and latitude) coloured by age minus their median, and a side plot of age against the cosine of the angle to an axis the player drags on the sky. The rms scatter about a straight-line fit in that side plot is shown live.
- The map's limits (B1240-64's period good to three figures, the map's distances off by up to six times, the height ticks without a sign) appear as notes on the readouts where they matter and in the codex result of `map.identify`.

## The five checkers (pure, in `checkers.ts`, on `epochModel(seed)`; every wrong answer reports what it computed)
Answer shapes are JSON (`Answer`); the instrument and the bots send exactly these through the bus.
1. `map.periods`, answer `{ line: order, jname }`: OK when `jname` is the best candidate of that line in `identification()` (pass 2), the line is old (not young), and its log10 odds are at least `ANCHOR_LOG10_MARGIN` (the identification's own anchor threshold). Wrong: the candidate's period now against the map's, its direction offset, its odds; a young line: "its period changes too fast to match by period alone".
2. `map.old`, answer `{ yearsAgo }` (from the spin-down clock): OK when `yearsAgo > 0` and it lies inside the union of the 95% intervals of the old lines matched so far, in the alien's frame (`ageOf(..., 'own')`, `lo` to `hi`). Wrong in the future: every matched pulsar ticks slower now than on the map, so the map cannot be from the future; wrong in the past: the nearest matched interval.
3. `map.identify`, answer `{ [order]: jname }` for all 14 lines: OK when every line's pulsar is that line's best candidate (pass 2). Wrong: list each line whose pulsar is not the most likely, with the odds of the best against it. Result readings: the 14 matches with their odds; the notes on the map's limits.
4. `map.motion`, answer `{ lDeg, bDeg }` (an axis on the sky): OK when `axisDeltaChi2(motionFit(), axis) <= CHI2_2DOF_95` (two angles at 95%; `triangulation.ts` exports the constant). `axisDeltaChi2` is new in `pulsarAge.ts`: the smallest Mahalanobis distance squared, over the speed s along the axis (closed form s = a'C^-1 v / a'C^-1 a), between the fit's velocity and s times the unit axis, with the fit's `velCov`. The axis is a line (s may be negative). Test it: 0 at the fit's own direction, rises as the axis turns, equals `velocityMahalanobis2` of the projected velocity under an isotropic covariance; check id `map.motionAxis`. Result readings: the fitted age and its sigma, the speed and direction of the alien's motion relative to the map's makers.
5. `map.region`, answer `{ x, y }` (pc, in the galactic plane, from the alien's star at the map epoch: where the player put the map's origin): OK when `insideRegion(region(), [x, y])`. Wrong: `regionLevel` of the point and the rms angle residual of the lines from it (`fitMapOrigin` with the origin fixed is not available; compute the residuals of the bearings from the proposed origin with the model's map points and the best rotation, in a small pure helper in `checkers.ts` with a test). Result readings: the region's size (2 x the semi-axes), how many catalogued stars lie inside it at the map epoch (`starInMapPlane` over `stars.near`), and whether the alien's own star is among them (D-A15's twist, foreshadowed: say it plainly, no conclusion about the Sun yet).

## Computing without freezing the page
