## The science you add to `kinematics.ts`

## The instrument (one screen at 1440x900 and 1366x768; follow the layout conventions the M3 layout pass left in `src/ui/` and the M3 chapters' CSS)
- **The neighbourhood chart** (`mountNeighbourhood`, `trail: model.trailRows()`) fills most of the instrument. Picking a star draws its trail (`showTrail`) and a readout: the angle between the craft's motion relative to that star and the direction from that star, with its error bar (alien unit for angles is degrees as elsewhere; follow `src/ui/units.ts`), the distance, the relative speed and distance over speed. A star without a radial velocity is drawn hollow (the chart does) and its readout says so honestly, with `minAngleNoRv` for plus or minus 100 km/s (and the escape-speed bound the test uses, if it uses one). "Tint all" (`showAll`) is available only after `trail.origin` is confirmed, as a way to see the result, not to find it.
- **The run-back** (after `trail.origin`): a time control that runs the craft and the chosen star back together; the separation shrinks to a meeting; the time at the meeting is what the player proposes.
- **The ruler** (after `trail.origin`): click the alien's own star and the origin star; the ruler shows the distance between them as the alien measures it, with its error (the projection of the measured position covariance on the line between them).
- **The region view** (after `trail.origin` and The Map's `map.region`): a plane view (SVG, the galactic plane, pc from the alien's star at the map epoch) of the map's origin region (`region()`: its 95% ellipse) and the stars of the near catalogue at the map epoch (`starInMapPlane` with `motionFit().ageYears`); the player clicks the star the map was drawn from.
- **The reframe** (after `trail.where` and The Map's `map.motion`): the old lines' ages from The Map in the alien's own frame (`ages('own')`), drawn as points with error bars, and a frame control: the alien's own frame, or the frame of any star the player picks. Moving into the origin star's frame uses `ages('sun')` (the Sun is the origin: the model reframes with `measured`); any other star's frame uses `datePulsar` with `reframe` set to the alien's measured state relative to that star (position and velocity minus the star's, same covariance), cached in a small pure helper with a test. In the origin star's frame the points snap together; the combined age and its interval appear only after the player proposes one.

## The five checkers (pure, in `checkers.ts`, on `epochModel(seed)`; every wrong answer reports what it computed)
1. `trail.origin`, answer `{ starId }`: OK when the star has a trail row, is not the alien's own star, and `angleDeg <= TRAIL_CONSISTENT_SIGMA * angleSigmaDeg`. Wrong: the angle and its error ("it moves at 37 +/- 2 degrees from straight away from that star", numbers computed); a star without a radial velocity: refused with `minAngleNoRv`. Result readings: the star, the angle and its error, the distance.
2. `trail.age`, answer `{ years }`: OK when `years` lies inside the origin row's `distOverSpeedInterval95`. Wrong: the interval. Result readings: distance over speed and its interval.
3. `trail.neighbour`, answer `{ distancePc }` (recorded from the ruler): OK when within two of the ruler's own computed sigmas of the alien's measured distance. On OK the message delivers D-A15's twist from the science: the origin star is the nearest catalogued star to the alien's (`nearestStars`) and, at its computed magnitude, the fifth-brightest in the alien's sky (`brightestStars`); say both with their computed numbers. Result readings: the distance, the rank by distance, the magnitude and its rank by brightness.
4. `trail.where`, answer `{ starId }`: OK when it is the origin star (the star of the confirmed `trail.origin`) and `insideRegion(region(), starInMapPlane(star, motionFit().ageYears))`. Wrong: another star inside the region ("inside the region too, one of N stars there; the machine did not come from it"), or a star outside (its `regionLevel`).
5. `trail.when`, answer `{ yearsAgo }` (proposed in the origin star's frame): OK when it lies inside `ages('sun').combined` `lo` to `hi`. Wrong: the interval, and if the player is still in another frame, the spread of the ages there. On OK the message adds the cross-check: the motion The Map fitted from the pulsars alone agrees with the origin star's measured velocity relative to the alien's star (`velocityMahalanobis2(motionFit(), measured.velKmS)`, as a plain "within N sigma"), and the travel time from `trail.age`. Result readings: the map's age and interval, the velocity agreement, the travel time.
