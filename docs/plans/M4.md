## The design rule from M3's review (applies to every M4 deduction)

**Deduce on the instrument, do not pick from a list.** The player selects evidence on the instruments
(clicks a pulsar on the chart, drags a map line onto a catalogue entry, sets a counting time, picks the
star on the chart) and the checker judges the selection with the science. Multiple-choice text answers
are a last resort. Every clue is the most legible thing on screen (M3 step 8b's targets).

## What the player does

**2. The Map (about 20 min).** The cover's map (`#map` in the cover SVG): 15 lines from one point, 14
carrying binary numbers. The alien's pulsar catalogue at the epoch (the real ATNF catalogue projected
44,100 years forward, `projectCatalogue`) on a 3D chart of the galaxy (three.js: the pulsars at their
positions, the Galactic centre, the alien's own star). The player:
- reads the 14 numbers through the key and recognises them as pulsar periods (they fall in the
  catalogue's range; The Key already showed this);
- notices nearly every map period is shorter than the matching catalogue period: the map is old;
- identifies the lines: drags each map line onto a pulsar on the chart (direction and latitude) and
  sees the match quality computed (the likelihood of D-A16: how sharply the candidate predicts the map's
  number, its direction and latitude); old pulsars first (sharp), then the young ones (Crab, Vela,
  B1727-47) by direction once the age is known roughly;
- dates the map from the old pulsars in the alien's own frame: the ages scatter by thousands of years
  (`dating.epoch.noReframe`): "something is wrong" (the alien's own motion), left open until The Trail;
- triangulates the origin: fits the map onto the chart and sees the region (about 31 by 14 pc, about a
  thousand stars, and the alien's own star among them in 1971: D-A15's twist foreshadowed).
- The map's limits go into the codex: B1240-64's period known only to three figures, distances off by
  up to six times, the height ticks without a sign.

**3. The Spot (about 5 min).** A faint radioactive spot on the cover that no diagram shows (its position
is a documented game choice: decide and log it as D-A23 before building). The player finds it with a
detector sweep, then counts alpha particles: the counting time sets the error bars (Poisson; longer
counts tighten them live); the U-234/U-238 ratio gives an age (`ageFromU234`); measuring Th-230 too
tests whether it started pure (`fitAgeAndPurity`, `testStartHypothesis`). A short count gives an honest
wide interval; assuming natural uranium fails against the second isotope.

**4. The Trail (about 10 min).** The craft's velocity against the alien's star catalogue (the trail
table, `kinematics.trail`): the player picks stars on a chart of the neighbourhood and sees, for each,
the angle between the craft's motion and the direction from that star; exactly one star has the craft
flying straight away from it (the Sun, within its measurement error; the stars without radial
velocities shown honestly). Distance over speed: about 44,100 years (a third clock). That star sits
inside the map's region: WHERE. Then the reframe: move the pulsar periods into that star's rest frame
(the Sun's velocity relative to the alien's star) and the map's ages snap together (`dating.epoch.reframe`):
WHEN, with the error bar honest under the stated model. The neighbour twist lands here: that star is the
alien's nearest neighbour, the fifth-brightest in its sky.

## Steps (specs in `docs/plans/M4_specs/` before delegating)
1. Decision D-A23: the uranium spot's position on the cover (documented choice; not on the NASA diagram).
2. The galaxy chart (three.js module, like the encounter scene: pulsars, the Galactic centre, the map's
   region, the alien's star; a handle for selection and projection), and the neighbourhood chart (the
   stars within about 10 pc of the craft at the epoch).
3. The Map chapter (identification by dragging lines onto pulsars; the unreframed dating; the
   triangulation; the limits).
4. The Spot chapter (detector sweep, alpha counting, the two-isotope test).
5. The Trail chapter (the trail on the neighbourhood chart, distance over speed, the reframe that snaps
   the map's ages together, WHERE and WHEN in the codex).
6. Graph and gating: the hub after The Key opens map, spot and trail; The Trail's reframe needs the map
   dated in the alien's frame first; deductions and hints for every new node (questions without
   spoilers).
7. Sound for the new moments (the ages snapping together; the Sun found); bots (right and wrong answers)
   through the new chapters; captures; the player-critic; Stewart's feedback from M3 folded in.
8. Gate: checks green, QA audit, PROGRESS and HANDOFF, `docs/plans/M5.md`.
